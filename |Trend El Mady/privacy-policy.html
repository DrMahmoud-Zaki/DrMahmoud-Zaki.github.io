Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Welcome to Trend El Mady.

This Privacy Policy explains how Trend El Mady handles information when users interact with our website, services, and social media publishing integrations.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the services used, Trend El Mady may process information required to operate its content publishing and automation systems. This may include account identifiers, authentication information, access tokens, video content, captions, and publishing information.

2. TikTok Integration

Trend El Mady may use TikTok's official APIs to connect an authorized TikTok account with our content publishing system.

When a user authorizes the integration, the application may process the permissions and information necessary to publish content to the authorized TikTok account.

We do not access TikTok information beyond the permissions required for the functionality provided by the application.

3. Video and Content Publishing

Content such as videos, captions, hashtags, and publishing information may be processed by our automation system in order to prepare and publish content to authorized social media platforms.

4. Authentication Information

Authentication credentials and access tokens are used only to provide the authorized integration and publishing functionality.

We do not sell authentication information or access tokens.

5. Data Sharing

Trend El Mady does not sell or rent users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services only when necessary to provide the requested functionality, including authorized social media APIs and automation or hosting services.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by our services and automation systems. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the intended functionality, maintain records, troubleshoot the system, or comply with applicable requirements.

8. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with their authorized integration by contacting Trend El Mady.

Where applicable, users may also revoke the application's access through the relevant third-party platform.

9. Third-Party Services

Trend El Mady may use third-party services and APIs, including social media platforms and hosting or automation services. Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to our services, integrations, or legal requirements. The updated version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests concerning Trend El Mady, please contact us through the official contact method provided on the Trend El Mady website.
